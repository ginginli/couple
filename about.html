About & editorial policy

zombie-couple.xyz is an independent AI video tool. We also publish a running comparison of zombie couple video generators, including the ones we compete with.

Who writes the guides

The guides are written and checked by the same small team that builds and runs this site. We do not claim outside expert credentials, a newsroom, or independent lab testing. Our author profile is public, and every guide carries its check date.

How prices and limits are checked

For each tool we read its own public pricing, FAQ and help pages on the date shown at the top of the guide. We record the one-video price, clip length, resolution, free allowance, account requirement, watermark and refund terms. If a site does not state a fact, we write “not stated” instead of guessing.

We did not buy or generate videos from the competing tools listed in the comparison, and the guide says so. That means we can compare published terms, not output quality. When a competitor is better on a specific point, we name that point.

How we handle our own product

zombie-couple.xyz appears in our comparison and is marked as ours. We disclose the conflict at the top of the guide, list our trade-offs, and do not use affiliate links or invented scores. See the full 10-tool comparison.

Corrections

Prices and limits change without notice. If something is out of date, send the URL and the correct figure through the contact page; we re-check it against the provider's page and update the check date when the change is confirmed.
