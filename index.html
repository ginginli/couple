What Is an AI Zombie Couple Video Generator?

            

              
An AI zombie couple video generator is a tool that turns two ordinary photos into a short vertical film. You upload the person (or pet) who gets infected, upload yourself, and the model builds a four-shot story around you: a close-up coming out of the fog, a standoff you cannot finish, a sprint with arms wide open, and a cut back to a sunny day before the outbreak.

              
It started as a short-video trend in late September — people call it the AI zombie effect or the zombie love story. Two versions spread the fastest: the zombie couple video where a partner is infected, and the pet version where an owner faces their infected cat or dog.

Frequently Asked Questions

              
            

            

              

                
                  How do I make the zombie couple video?
                  +
                
                
Upload a photo of your partner first and one of yourself second, pick a world and generate. Your partner walks out of the fog infected, you lower the gun, they charge at you, and the clip cuts to a sunny day where the same run turns into a laughing hug. A clip takes about four to five minutes.

              

              

                
                  Which photo goes first?
                  +
                
                
The order decides who is who. Photo 1 is the one who has been infected; photo 2 is the survivor holding the gun, usually you. Swap them and you become the infected one instead.

              

              

                
                  Can I use my dog or cat?
                  +
                
                
Yes, as photo 1. Your pet comes out of the fog with dusty fur and clouded eyes, races toward you, and in the sunny version knocks you over for a cuddle. Use a clear photo where the whole animal is visible.

              

              

                
                  Is it gory?
                  +
                
                
No. The gun is never fired: the survivor lowers it and lets it drop. There is no blood and no biting. The infected look is ashen, veined skin and milky eyes on a face you still recognize, and the clip ends in a warm, laughing hug.

              

              

                
                  How long is it, and what shape?
                  +
                
                
Thirteen seconds, vertical 9:16, with sound: a tense heartbeat that turns into a soft piano at the cut. It renders at 720p by default; you can go up to 1080p for the sharpest output.

              

              

                
                  How many credits does it cost?
                  +
                
                
600 credits (≈ $3.00) at 720p and 2,100 (≈ $10.50) at 1080p. 1 credit = $0.005, so 200 credits = $1. Credits are sold in packs, never per video; the Generate button shows the cost of each render.

              

              

                
                  Will it look exactly like them?
                  +
                
                
Close enough to tell who it is, but not pixel-perfect. Clear, front-facing photos keep the likeness best.

              

              

                
                  What is the AI zombie trend?
                  +
                
                
A short-video format that went viral at the end of September. In a grey world after an outbreak, someone raises a gun at the person they love most, cannot shoot, and gets pulled into a hug as the clip cuts to a happier day.

              

              

                
                  Whose photos can I use?
                  +
                
                
Adults and pets only, and only people who are happy to star in it. Clear, front-facing photos with the face fully visible work best.
