Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-21

This policy explains what data zombie-couple.xyz collects when you use the AI zombie couple video generator, why we collect it, and how you can have it deleted.

Information we collect

Photos you upload: the two images you place in the generator slots, used only to render the video you request.

Account and contact data: your email address if you create an account, subscribe, or write to support.

Payment data: handled entirely by our payment provider (Stripe). We never see or store full card numbers.

Usage data: pages viewed, credits spent, and anonymous analytics events that help us fix bugs.

How we use your photos

Uploaded photos are sent to our video model provider only to produce the clip you asked for. They are not published, not sold, and not used to train models.

Source photos and intermediate frames are deleted from processing servers within 30 days. Rendered videos are removed 90 days after generation unless you save them to your account.

Consent and eligible content

Only upload photos of consenting adults, your own children with parental responsibility, or your own pets. Do not upload photos of people who have not agreed to appear.

Cookies and analytics

We use a privacy-friendly analytics tool to count visits and errors. We do not run advertising or cross-site tracking cookies at launch. If we add advertising later, this page will be updated first.

Third-party services

Stripe — payments; Vercel / Cloudflare — hosting and CDN; our video model provider — rendering; our analytics provider — traffic measurement. Each processes data under its own privacy policy.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete your account, uploads and generated videos. Requests are answered within 30 days.

Depending on where you live you may also have rights under GDPR (EU/UK) or CCPA (California). Write to us and we will honour the applicable request.

Children

The service is intended for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

When this policy changes we update the date at the top of the page. Material changes are also announced on the site.
